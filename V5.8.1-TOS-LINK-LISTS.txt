420VaultBot v5.8.1

TOS quiz fixes
- Every quiz question is rendered in a fresh Discord embed.
- The question remains visible with its answer dropdown.
- Five questions are selected from the larger question bank per attempt.
- Question selection/order and answer order are randomized.
- The immediately previous quiz sequence is not reused for that member in the running process.

Unified link-list loading
- On first ready for a guild, the bot discovers and imports shipped list files into the managed FTS index.
- Recognized names: list.txt, lists.txt, links_part_N.txt, lists_part_N.txt.
- Searches use the unified deduplicated database index, so all imported parts contribute to 420_search.
- Existing shipped legacy files lists.txt, lists_part_2.txt, lists_part_3.txt and lists_part_4.txt are recognized.

Scraper continuation parts
- A successful web scrape is automatically saved under data/link_lists/links_part_N.txt.
- N is selected after the highest existing list/links part number, so the current legacy parts 1-4 lead to links_part_5.txt.
- Each successful future scrape advances to part 6, 7, 8, etc.
- The new part is automatically normalized, deduplicated and indexed for 420_search.
