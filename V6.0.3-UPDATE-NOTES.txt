Link Library & Scraper update:

- Uploaded URL lists are normalized, deduplicated, persisted as the next data/link_lists/links_part_N.txt, indexed, and immediately searchable.

- Follows XML sitemap indexes, including .xml.gz where available.
- Extracts public HTTP/HTTPS content URLs, persists a new list part, indexes it, and reloads search.
- Existing crawler remains available for crawlable websites.
- Does not bypass anti-bot protections.
