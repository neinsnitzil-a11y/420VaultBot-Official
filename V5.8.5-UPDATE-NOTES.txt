420VaultBot v5.8.5

AUDIO AUDITION UX
- WAV and MP3 are the only playable Vault formats.
- Removed automatic first-result upload when a search/page opens.
- One click on a WAV/MP3 result immediately attaches/replaces the native Discord audio player on the existing browser message.
- No Preview button and no extra Selected confirmation for audio.
- Presets such as FST remain non-audio and are download-only.
- Browser shows the currently auditioned filename.
- Download Original / Selected remains separate from auditioning.

DISCORD LIMITATION
Discord native audio players are message attachments. Discord does not allow a bot to render the native player inside an embed/select-menu body or force autoplay. This build uses the closest supported one-click audition workflow without posting a separate preview message.
