420VaultBot v5.5.4 — Portable Recommended Roles

Create Required Roles now creates/maps the full recommended portable set when missing:
- TOS Accepted
- Verified
- Unverified
- Paid Member
- Beta Tester

The bot stores role IDs after creation/mapping. It does not create Producer, Artist, Listener, Owner, or unrelated roles. Server owner and Discord Administrator permissions remain name-independent admin bypasses.

TOS acceptance assigns both TOS Accepted and Verified. Re-verification removes both until the current Terms are accepted again.
