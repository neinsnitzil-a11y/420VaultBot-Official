420VaultBot v5.5.5 - Independent Heartbeat / Windows Background Runtime

- Dedicated daemon thread checks bot readiness every 30 seconds by default.
- Writes data/heartbeat.json atomically for external health inspection.
- On Windows, SetThreadExecutionState requests ES_SYSTEM_REQUIRED while the bot
  is running. ES_DISPLAY_REQUIRED is intentionally NOT used, so the screen may
  turn off while Windows keeps the system awake.
- Configure with HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_SECONDS and HEARTBEAT_PREVENT_SYSTEM_SLEEP.
- run_420vault_watchdog.bat restarts the Python process 10 seconds after an
  unexpected exit.
- This cannot keep the bot alive through shutdown, hibernation, power loss,
  Internet loss, or a lid-close policy that forces sleep/hibernate/shutdown.
